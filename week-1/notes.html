What this page is, and how to write in it

Week 1 runs across two lectures. Each tab keeps its own notes and its own date, saved in this browser beside the highlights on the reading guide.

What each author argues goes in the notes by text on the reading guide. This page is for what was said in the room.

Type straight into a tab and it saves on its own. Cmd or Ctrl + B for bold, + I for italic, + H for the last colour you used, and again to take it off. Selecting text brings up a small bar with bold, italic, a bullet list and the colours. Typing - or 1. starts a list, ### starts a heading, and Tab inside a list indents.

Left and right arrow keys move between the two tabs.

Everything you write here stays in this browser. These notes, the reading guide's notes, and every highlight and comment on it are one saved bundle on the device you are using. They survive a reload and a closed tab, they are not sent anywhere, and clearing your browsing data removes them.
