How these work, and when to use them

A set is built in two passes, and the tag on each question says which pass it came from. Questions marked from the reading are written from the chapters and can be answered from them. Questions marked from the lecture come from her posted notes and appear after the class has happened.

So the same page does two jobs. Do the reading questions the day you read, before the lecture. Come back after the lecture for the ones that were added, and again in November for the whole thing.

  1. Read the chapter, then do that part cold. Guessing before you know and then being corrected beats studying first.
  2. Write short answers out before opening the model. Reading a model answer you have not attempted teaches close to nothing, and the short answers are where the marks move on an exam that has them.
  3. After the lecture, come back for the new questions. They are the ones that tell you where the weight actually falls.
  4. In November, run the whole page again. Re-testing something you answered weeks ago is the highest-value hour available.
